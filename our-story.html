Our Story

Great Cooking Begins With Exceptional Ingredients

CT Artisanal Pantry began with a simple belief — and a family's move to Madagascar that changed how we think about flavour.

How It Started

A Pantry Worth Trusting

Four years ago, we made Madagascar our home. As a family who loves cooking together, we found the ingredients there unlike anything we'd worked with before — starting with the first vanilla bean we scraped for our favourite chocolate banana milkshake. That discovery became a pantry: a small, considered collection of ingredients we'd proudly cook with ourselves.

Since moving to Madagascar, we have travelled across the country and fallen in love with its gentle-natured, smiling people — full of potential, yet living in some of the harshest environments. As a family interested in experimenting with and cooking home-cooked meals to share with friends and family, we found the ingredients in Madagascar to be unique. We still remember the first time we saw the vanilla bean and scraped the seeds to make our favourite chocolate banana milkshake.

Living close to, and travelling through, some of the most remarkable growing regions in Madagascar revealed something powerful — the quality of an ingredient changes everything.

A modern pantry rooted in landscape and origin.

CT Artisanal Pantry curates exceptional ingredients from remarkable landscapes where climate, soil, and craft come together to create extraordinary flavour. Born in Madagascar and inspired by the world's great growing regions, our pantry brings together ingredients that reflect the places they come from.

Pure ingredients. Distinctive landscapes. Remarkable flavour.

Our initial collection remains intentionally small. Every product we offer must meet one simple standard: it must be something we would proudly cook ourselves. From origin to kitchen, our goal is to make exceptional ingredients accessible to those who care about how food tastes, feels, and connects us.

Origins

Where Flavour Begins

Climate, soil, and cultivation shape the character of every ingredient in our pantry. From Madagascar's fertile landscapes to remarkable growing regions around the world, each ingredient reflects the place it comes from.

Why It Matters

Traceable, By Design

We work directly with growers rather than brokers, so every ingredient can be traced back to a specific region — not just a country of origin on a label.
